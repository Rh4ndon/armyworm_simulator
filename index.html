4 run modes (add borers first — press Seed Farm or use the Infest tool, or these buttons won't run):
▸ Run Daily — no control; keeps going until the pest is out of control (≥80% infestation) or the season ends.
▸ Run + Pheromone 🪤 — auto-places sex-pheromone traps (catch male moths, disrupt mating), runs until harvest.
▸ Run + Spray 💧 — farmer auto-sprays the whole plot in one day when ≥5% threshold, repeats every ~7 days, until harvest.
▸ Run + Lure & Spray 🪤💧 — combined IPM: pheromone traps and whole-farm threshold sprays (every ~10 days, fewer sprays) and weekly hand-removal of damaged shoots/fruit, until harvest. The three together beat either alone.

Harvest 🍆 starts 46–50 days after transplanting, picking every 3–4 days (~2×/week) over a ~120-day profitable season (PH DA + Bt-eggplant trials). All run modes simulate until the pest is out of control (≥80%), goes extinct, or the season ends; manual +1 Day is capped at 120 days, then the report opens.

Farm layout (.csv): the field comes from farm.csv — one comma-separated row per line; a cell containing plant is an eggplant, anything else (e.g. farm path) is a walkway. Edit farm.csv and refresh, or use Load Farm (.csv) to try your own field — the grid resizes to the file.

Sim Speed ×: only changes how fast the days tick (a viewing pace) — it does not change pest biology or the outcome. Use it to watch a run faster or slower.

Spray 💧: one spray day covers every plant on the plot (a farmer sprays the whole field in a day). Kills exposed eggs/larvae, ~5-day residue. Larvae are only vulnerable a few hours after hatching (evening sprays work best).

Lure 🪤: water-trap with a 4 mg pheromone lure ~1 m high attracts male moths (mass trapping) to suppress mating.

Combined 🪤💧 (research): a 10-day spray interval + pheromone traps + hand removal gave 95.96% shoot / 94.85% fruit reduction — the best IPM package tested (IJACR 2021, Bangladesh). Traps also act as early-warning monitors. Note: even the best programme suppresses borers, it does not wipe them out — larvae sheltered inside fruit/shoots survive sprays and moths reinfest from nearby fields.
